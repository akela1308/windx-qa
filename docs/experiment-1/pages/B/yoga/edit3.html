The room

Wood, windows and the smell of bread

The studio sits above a bakery, so on a morning class the room smells of whatever is coming out of the oven downstairs. The floor is wood, the windows are big, and there are only 14 mats.

I am Mei, and I teach most of the classes. Two other teachers cover the rest. We would rather you leave feeling slower than when you came in.
