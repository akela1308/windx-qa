## Art direction (mandatory): Warm editorial

A thoughtful magazine. Warm cream or sand paper, dark brown or green ink, one earthy accent, elegant serif headlines, long readable text, pull quotes, lots of air.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 86-96%, saturation 15-55%, hue 25-55.
- displayFont one of: fraunces, newsreader, instrument-serif, cormorant, young-serif. bodyFont one of: karla, work-sans, public-sans, source-serif, newsreader.
- radius 0-8px.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.statement, story.longform, services.prose-columns, proof.single-quote. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
