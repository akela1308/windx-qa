What we do

Three areas, done carefully

We keep our work focused so that every client gets someone who does this every day.

Family law

Divorce, separation and arrangements for children. We help you understand your options, agree what we can without a fight, and speak up for you in court when it cannot be avoided.

Wills and lasting powers of attorney

A will makes sure your wishes are followed. A lasting power of attorney lets someone you trust make decisions for you if you cannot. We write both in plain language and walk you through each step.

Probate

When someone has died, there is a lot to do at a time when you least want to do it. We deal with the paperwork and the authorities and keep you informed throughout.

Questions

Things people often ask us

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The first call lasts 20 minutes and costs nothing.

Do you always charge a fixed fee?

Where we can, yes. Some matters, such as a dispute that may end up in court, are harder to price in advance. We will tell you which kind yours is.

Can you help with both a divorce and arrangements for the children?

Yes. Family law is one of our main areas and covers divorce, separation and arrangements for children.

What is a lasting power of attorney?

It is a legal document that lets someone you trust make decisions for you if you are unable to. We can talk through the options on your first call.

Can you help after someone has died?

Yes. Probate is one of our areas of work, and we can explain what needs doing and in what order.
