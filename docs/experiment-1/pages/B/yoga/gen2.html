The room

Small on purpose

I'm Mei. I teach most of the classes here, with two other teachers. We keep the room to fourteen mats so nobody is lost at the back and everyone gets a word, a hand or a quiet nod.

The floor is wood, the windows are big, and in the mornings the whole studio smells of bread from the bakery downstairs. We want it to feel like a room you come back to, not a gym and not a brand.

New to yoga

The four week beginners course

You come to the same class once a week with the same small group, so by week two you know the room and the people in it. No experience, no flexibility and no special clothes needed.

  1. 1

    Arrive

    Mats, props, how to breathe, and a few simple shapes.

  2. 2

    Move

    Linking shapes slowly, and finding what feels right in your body.

  3. 3

    Stay

    Longer holds, a little more strength, a lot more ease.

  4. 4

    Carry on

    A full gentle class, and a plan for joining the regular timetable.
