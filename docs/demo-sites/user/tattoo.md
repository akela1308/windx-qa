Ink Arch is a private tattoo studio in a railway arch near Camden Market. Three artists: Mira does fine line and botanicals, Jay does traditional, Sol does blackwork. By appointment only, consultations free, deposits to book. Strict hygiene, no walk-in flash. We want the site to show each artist's style and get people to request a consultation.

## Art direction (mandatory): Night shift

After hours. Near-black background, off-white text, one vivid accent (acid lime, safety orange, hot red or electric cyan). Condensed or wide grotesque headlines, hard edges, a workshop or club energy.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 3-13%, saturation 0-30%.
- displayFont one of: big-shoulders, archivo, space-grotesk, syne, unbounded. bodyFont one of: ibm-plex-sans, work-sans, space-grotesk, public-sans, ibm-plex-mono.
- radius 0-6px.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.type-poster, signature.marquee, services.ledger, contact.big-type. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
