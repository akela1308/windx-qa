Kiln Yoga is a small studio above a bakery in Peckham, only 14 mats so classes stay small. I'm Mei, I teach most classes with two other teachers. We do slow flow, yin, a 4 week beginners course, and pregnancy yoga on Saturday mornings. The room is warm, wooden floor, big windows, smells of bread in the mornings. People can book single classes or a class pass. I want the site to feel like the room: unhurried, warm, a bit handmade, not like a gym and not like the glossy yoga brands.

## Art direction (mandatory): Soft clay

Soft, tactile and friendly. A pastel tinted background (sage, blush, pale clay, mint, butter; not beige and not lilac-blue), rounded shapes, friendly rounded or soft serif type, gentle illustrations, notes and stamps.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 80-93%, saturation 20-70%.
- displayFont one of: bricolage, young-serif, fraunces, syne. bodyFont one of: karla, work-sans, bricolage, public-sans.
- radius 14-32px.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.split-photo, signature.note, signature.custom-svg, services.feature-plus-list. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
