Little Fern is a neighbourhood cafe on Acre Lane in Brixton. Speciality coffee from a small Bristol roaster, breakfast all day, sourdough toasties, cakes baked by Fern's mum. Lots of laptops on weekdays, families on weekends, dogs welcome. Open 7.30am to 4pm, closed Mondays. Want the site to show the menu and feel cosy, not trendy.

## Art direction (mandatory): One colour

Everything in one hue: a light-to-mid tint of a single colour as background (terracotta, sage, ochre, slate teal, rose) and a deep shade of the same hue for text. Calm, designed, unmistakable.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 62-86%, saturation 20-65%.
- displayFont one of: fraunces, instrument-serif, bricolage, young-serif, space-grotesk, dm-serif. bodyFont one of: karla, work-sans, public-sans, source-serif.
- radius 0-20px.
- tokens.colors.ink must be a deep shade of the background hue (within 25 degrees).
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.statement, hero.offset-photo, story.letter, services.feature-plus-list. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
