How the first call goes

Do I need to have my papers ready?

No. Tell us what has happened in your own order. We ask the questions that matter and write nothing down that you have not said.

Will you tell me what it costs?

Yes, before the call ends. Where the work is clear we quote a fixed fee. Where it is not, we say why and give you the hourly rate.

What if I only want to ask one thing?

Then that is the call. Twenty minutes is yours, and nobody will push you to sign up.

What happens if I decide to go ahead?

We send you a short letter with the fee and the first three steps. After that, one of us is your point of contact.
