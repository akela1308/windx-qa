What you are probably wondering, and what we do about it

I do not even know where to begin.

Book the free 20 minute call. You tell us what has happened, we tell you your options in plain words. There is nothing to prepare and no obligation to go further.

What is this going to cost me?

Where we can, we quote a fixed fee. Where we cannot, we explain why, give you an estimate, and tell you before costs move beyond it.

Will I have to deal with a stranger each time?

No. One of the two partners looks after your matter, helped by our paralegal. You will always know who has your file.

What happens after we decide to go ahead?

We confirm the fee and the plan in a short letter, then take it step by step. After each stage we tell you what is done and what comes next.

A small practice, on purpose

Hartwell & Okafor is two partners, Clare Hartwell and Tunde Okafor, and a paralegal. We work from Islington, and most of the people we help live nearby.

Most people come to us at a difficult time: a marriage ending, a parent who has died, a will that has been put off for years. They are often worried about what it will cost and what they will be asked to do.

So we keep things plain. We explain the law without jargon, we say what will happen next, and we agree the fee early. You should never have to chase us to find out where your matter stands.

What clients say

Common questions

Is the first call really free?

Yes. It lasts 20 minutes, it is free, and you are under no obligation to instruct us afterwards.

Do you charge fixed fees?

Where we can. Many wills, powers of attorney and straightforward matters have a fixed fee we agree with you in advance. For work that is harder to predict, we explain why and give an estimate.

What should I bring to the first call?

Nothing is required. If you have a few key documents to hand, such as a marriage certificate or an existing will, it can help, but we can start without them.

Do I have to go to court?

Not always. Many family matters are settled by agreement. We will tell you honestly if court looks likely and what that would involve.

Can you help if I do not live in Islington?

Yes. Many conversations can happen by phone or video, and we will tell you when a meeting in person would help.
