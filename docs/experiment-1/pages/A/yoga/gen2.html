Classes

What we teach

Four kinds of class, all small, all unhurried.

  1. 01Slow flowMovement linked with breath, at a pace where you can actually notice what you are doing. Mixed levels are welcome.
  2. 02YinLong, quiet holds on the floor with support. Time to let the body soften and the head go quiet.
  3. 03Beginners courseFour weeks with the same small group, learning the basics step by step so the room stops feeling new.
  4. 04Pregnancy yogaA gentle class for pregnant students, on Saturday mornings.

The room

A studio that feels like a room, not a gym

We are up a flight of stairs above a bakery. In the mornings the whole studio smells of bread, which is a good way to start a practice and an even better way to finish one.

The floor is wood, the windows are big, and there are only 14 mats. That is on purpose. With a small group, nobody is hidden at the back and nobody is shouted at from a stage.

Mei teaches most of the classes, with two other teachers sharing the week. We would rather you leave feeling a bit slower than a bit wrung out.

Small classes, a warm room, no rush.

First time

If you have never done yoga before

I am not flexible. Is this for me?

Yes. Flexibility is something yoga gives you, not something you bring. We show options for every pose.

Where should I start?

The 4 week beginners course. Same small group, same room, a little more each week.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes. We have mats, and you can leave your shoes at the door.

Booking

Two ways to book

Class pass

Questions

Before you come

How many people are in a class?

There are 14 mats in the room, so never more than 14 students.

Can I do pregnancy yoga at any stage?

Please tell Mei how far along you are when you book, and check with your midwife or doctor if you are unsure.

Do I need to book?

Yes, please book a mat. Single classes and class passes are both bookable online.

Which class is best when I am tired?

Yin. Mostly lying down, fully supported, and very forgiving.
