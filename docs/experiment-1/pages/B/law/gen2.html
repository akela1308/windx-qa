What we do

Three areas, done properly.

We keep our work narrow on purpose. It means the person advising you has handled your kind of situation many times before.

  1. i.

    Family law

    Divorce, separation and the practical questions that follow: where the children will live, how time is shared, and how money and the home are divided. We aim to agree things calmly wherever that is possible, and we are ready to go to court when it is not.

    • Divorce and separation
    • Arrangements for children
    • Agreeing how to divide money and property
  2. ii.

    Wills and lasting powers of attorney

    A will says who should have what and who looks after your children. A lasting power of attorney lets someone you trust make decisions if you cannot. We explain each choice in plain English, so you know what you are signing.

    • Wills for individuals and couples
    • Lasting powers of attorney, property and finance
    • Lasting powers of attorney, health and welfare
  3. iii.

    Probate

    When someone has died, there is a lot to do and little energy to do it. We deal with the paperwork, the bank and the estate, and tell you what is happening at each stage, so you can concentrate on your family.

    • Applying for the grant of probate
    • Collecting in and distributing the estate
    • Advice when there is no will

How working with us goes

No surprises, from first call to final letter.

Most people have never needed a solicitor before. This is what to expect, in order.

  1. Step one

    A free 20 minute call

    You tell us what is happening. We listen, tell you honestly what your options are, and say whether we are the right firm to help. If we are not, we will say so.

  2. Step two

    A written quote

    Where the work can be priced in advance, you get a fixed fee in writing. Where it cannot, we explain why and give you a clear estimate and a way to keep it under control.

  3. Step three

    We get on with it

    You have one named person to contact. We tell you what we are doing and what we need from you, in short plain emails and calls.

  4. Step four

    Finished, and still reachable

    When the matter is done, we confirm what has been completed and what, if anything, you need to do next. You can still call us with a question afterwards.
