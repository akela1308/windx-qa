Independent spreadsheet consulting in two parts: cleaning up a sheet you already have, or building a small working example of a task you repeat by hand.

Two services, and how to choose

Start with what you have in front of you: a sheet that has drifted, or a task you keep repeating.

Spreadsheet cleanup

Choose this when the sheet already exists and has become hard to trust. Names are written one way in one row and another way in the next. Dates are entered in different formats. The same row appears more than once. Cleanup makes names and dates consistent and identifies the duplicate rows, so the whole sheet reads the same way from top to bottom.

Workflow prototypes

Choose this when the sheet is fine but one task is repeated by hand, again and again. I build a small working example of that single task, then we discuss it before anything is implemented. You see how it behaves on a sample first, and decide afterwards whether it is worth taking further.

See the three steps

Three steps from sample to decision

  1. Discuss a sample

    You share a small sample of the sheet or the task, with anything sensitive taken out. We talk through what repeats and what should change.

  2. Review an example

    You receive a small working example built on that sample. It covers one repeated task and nothing more, so it is easy to check.

  3. Decide what to implement

    We discuss the example together. Only then do you decide what, if anything, is worth implementing.
