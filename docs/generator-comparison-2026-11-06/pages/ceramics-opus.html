The beginner session

Hand-building for beginners

No experience needed. In two hours you move from a lump of clay to a small vessel of your own: first getting to know the clay, then forming a simple shape, then choosing how its surface will be finished.

Three parts, two hours

  1. Get to know the clay

    Press, roll and pinch it to feel how soft it is and how it holds a shape.

  2. Form a simple vessel

    Build a small bowl or cup by hand, wall by wall.

  3. Choose a finish

    Decide how the surface should look and feel once your vessel is formed.

Run your thumb round the rim of a hand-built cup and you can feel every press your fingers made in the clay.
