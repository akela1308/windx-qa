Three areas, done carefully

We keep our work narrow on purpose, so the person you speak to has done this many times before.

Family law

Divorce, separation and arrangements for children. We help you reach an agreement where that is possible, and we stand firmly beside you where it is not. Fixed fees are available for many family matters.

Wills and lasting powers of attorney

A clear will so your wishes are followed, and a lasting power of attorney so someone you trust can act for you if you cannot. We write both in plain English and walk you through every line.

Probate

When someone close to you has died, we deal with the paperwork and the legal steps so you can focus on family. We tell you what happens next and roughly how long each stage takes.

What you are probably wondering

I have never spoken to a solicitor. What happens on the first call?

We give you 20 minutes, free. You tell us where things stand, we listen, and we say honestly whether we can help and what the sensible next step is. There is no pressure to go further.

What is this going to cost me?

Where the work can be priced in advance, we quote a fixed fee in writing before we start. Where it cannot, we explain why and give you a clear estimate.

Will I have to deal with a different person each time?

No. Clare, Tunde or our paralegal will know your file, and you will have a direct way to reach them.

What if I am not ready to decide anything yet?

That is fine. Many people call us just to understand their options. We will go at your pace.
