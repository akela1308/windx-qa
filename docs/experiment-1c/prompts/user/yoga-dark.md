Kiln Yoga is a small studio above a bakery in Peckham, only 14 mats so classes stay small. I'm Mei, I teach most classes with two other teachers. We do slow flow, yin, a 4 week beginners course, and pregnancy yoga on Saturday mornings. The room is warm, wooden floor, big windows, smells of bread in the mornings. People can book single classes or a class pass. I want the site to feel like the room: unhurried, warm, a bit handmade, not like a gym and not like the glossy yoga brands.

## Art direction (mandatory): Deep ink

Quiet confidence. A deep saturated background (forest green, navy, oxblood or aubergine), light warm text, a light or italic serif, wide spacing, very little decoration. Calm and premium, never flashy.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 8-26%, saturation 22-80%.
- displayFont one of: cormorant, instrument-serif, fraunces, newsreader, dm-serif. bodyFont one of: karla, public-sans, work-sans, source-serif, ibm-plex-sans.
- radius 0-6px.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.statement, story.principles, services.prose-columns, process.conversation. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
