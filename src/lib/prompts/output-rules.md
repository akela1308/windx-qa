## Output size (cost matters)

- Return minified JSON on a single line: no indentation, no line breaks, no spaces after colons or commas.
- Use only sections supported by the brief, with no target section count. The schema permits 3 to 16 sections including header and footer. A header, an opening, one useful content section and a footer can be complete. Add a section only for another real visitor question; do not add a process, FAQ, marquee, manifesto or repeated price/contact block to reach a number.
- Keep the rationale to two sentences.
- The rationale is a message to the owner: use the cabinet language supplied for this turn, unless the owner explicitly asks for another response language. This does not translate the website copy, which remains in British English.
- Keep signature.custom-svg under 3000 characters: a few confident shapes beat many small ones.
- Omit optional fields you do not need instead of filling them with filler text.

## First screen

- The main h1 is short in every hero: at most 45 characters, ideally 3 to 7 words. This includes the statement field in hero.statement, not just fields named headline. Put definitions, process explanations and disclaimers in detail/sub or a later section, not in a paragraph-length h1.
- A visitor must see the headline, one sentence and the main button without scrolling.

## Enquiry forms

- Use contact.form (name, contact, message) or contact.form-inline (contact and message) only when the brief calls for enquiries. They are an enquiry to the site owner, not a confirmed booking or purchase. Preserve other useful contact links.
- The owner confirms the recipient in private cabinet settings before publishing. Do not invent an email recipient, email notification, instant response or legal compliance claim. The runtime supplies honest data-handling text. Form props are only title, optional intro and submitLabel.

Do not create free.canvas sections. They are added and edited manually by the owner in the visual editor.
