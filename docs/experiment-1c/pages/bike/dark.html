Old steel frames

Wheel building

Rosa and Dami build wheels to suit how you ride, from a courier's daily slog to a commuter's quiet loop. Each one is laced by hand and trued on the stand before it leaves.

A steel diamond frame on the stand, the way it arrives and the way it leaves.

Hello, I'm Dami.

Most of what comes through the arch is a commuter bike with a flat or a rubbing brake. Rosa and I fix those while you wait, and you ride off.

What I really love is old steel. A frame that has sat in a shed for years can be stripped, repaired and put back on the road, and I will happily spend days on one.

If you are a courier or you ride in every day, tell us when you need the bike back and we plan the day around it.

Booking, or just turning up

Can I just turn up with a puncture?

Yes. Punctures and brake fixes are done while you wait, as long as we are not in the middle of another job.

I need the bike back today.

Tell us when you drop it off. Book a service in the morning and we will aim to have it ready for the ride home.

I don't know what is wrong with it.

Wheel it in. We look at it with you, say what it needs and what it costs, and only then pick up a spanner.

How do I book?

Ring the workshop or press the button below. We will find you a slot.

Drop it off on your way to work, ride it home tonight .
