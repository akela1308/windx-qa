I run a bike repair workshop called Lowgear Cycle Works. We're in a railway arch in Hackney, just off Mare Street. It's me (Dami) and one mechanic, Rosa. We do servicing, puncture and brake fixes while you wait, proper wheel building, and we restore old steel frames, that's the bit I love. Lots of our customers are couriers and commuters who need the bike back the same day. I want a site where people can see what we do and roughly what it costs, and book a service or just turn up.

## Art direction (mandatory): Swiss poster

International typographic style. White background, one strong signal colour (red, orange, cobalt or green), big grotesque type, strict grid, generous white space, uppercase small labels, no decoration except rules and numbers.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 96-100%, saturation 0-6%.
- displayFont one of: archivo, space-grotesk, libre-franklin, public-sans, bricolage. bodyFont one of: public-sans, libre-franklin, ibm-plex-sans, work-sans, karla.
- radius 0-4px.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.type-poster, services.index, process.steps-row, story.numbers. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
