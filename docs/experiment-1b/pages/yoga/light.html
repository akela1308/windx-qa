Hello, I'm Mei

I teach most of the classes here, with two other teachers who share the same slow way of working. I wanted a room that feels like a friend's front room, not a gym and not a showroom. So the studio is small on purpose: fourteen mats, a wooden floor, windows that let the day in. Some mornings the bakery downstairs sends the smell of fresh bread up the stairs. We have never minded.

Mei, founder and teacher

Never been to a class? Ask us anything

I have never done yoga. Will I be lost?

No. The beginners course is made for you, and in every class the teacher can reach every mat. Tell us it is your first time and we will look after you.

What do I need to bring?

Comfortable clothes and yourself. We have mats, blocks, bolsters and blankets in the studio. Bring your own mat if you prefer.

How do I book?

Choose a class, book a single place, or buy a class pass if you plan to come regularly. Classes are small, so booking ahead is wise.

I am pregnant. Can I come?

Yes, the Saturday morning class is for you. If you are unsure about anything, send us a message first and we will talk it through.

Come a little early, take your shoes off, and sit by the window. There is no rush here. See you on the mat. MeiMei

Practical things

How many people are in a class?

No more than 14, because that is how many mats fit in the room. That keeps every class calm and personal.

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend it. Places are limited to 14 mats, and the Saturday pregnancy class and the beginners course tend to fill first.

What is the class pass?

A bundle of classes you can use across slow flow, yin and the other classes on the timetable. Ask us for the current options.

Is the beginners course a good place to start?

Yes. It runs for 4 weeks with the same small group, so you learn the basics at an easy pace and get to know the room.
