Kiln Yoga is a small studio above a bakery in Peckham, only 14 mats so classes stay small. I'm Mei, I teach most classes with two other teachers. We do slow flow, yin, a 4 week beginners course, and pregnancy yoga on Saturday mornings. The room is warm, wooden floor, big windows, smells of bread in the mornings. People can book single classes or a class pass. I want the site to feel like the room: unhurried, warm, a bit handmade, not like a gym and not like the glossy yoga brands.

## Art direction (mandatory): Two-colour poster

A screen-printed poster in two strong colours: a bold coloured background and a contrasting second colour for headlines and accents. Playful type, stickers, marquee or stamp, a hand-made illustration. Joyful but still organised.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 45-80%, saturation 45-100%.
- displayFont one of: bricolage, syne, unbounded, young-serif, dm-serif. bodyFont one of: karla, work-sans, bricolage, public-sans.
- radius 0-28px.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.band, signature.custom-svg, signature.stamp, signature.marquee. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
