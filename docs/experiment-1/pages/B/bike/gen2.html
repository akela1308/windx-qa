Dami's favourite bit

Old steel, made rideable again

A good steel frame can outlive its owner. We restore them: strip back the old paint and rust, check the tubes and dropouts, and rebuild the bike with parts that suit it.

  1. Look it overWe check the frame for cracks, dents and rust through, and tell you honestly if it is worth the work.
  2. Strip it downEvery component off, frame bare, old grease and grime gone.
  3. Treat the steelRust dealt with, tubes protected inside and out.
  4. Build it backWheels, drivetrain, brakes and finishing, set up for how you ride.

Wheel building

Built by hand, spoke by spoke

A wheel is only as good as its tension.

Heavy loads, potholes and daily miles are hard on wheels. We build to suit the rider and the roads: pick the rim and hub with you, lace it, tension it and stress-relieve it so it stays true.

Already have a wobble? A true takes minutes and often saves a rebuild.
