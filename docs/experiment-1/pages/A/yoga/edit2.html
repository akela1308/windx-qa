Who teaches here

A room small enough to feel looked after

I'm Mei, and I teach most of the classes at Kiln with two other teachers. We keep the room to 14 mats on purpose, so nobody is lost at the back and nobody is rushed.

The floor is wood, the windows are big, and in the mornings the bakery downstairs makes the whole studio smell of bread. It is warm in every sense. We like it that way.

We are not a gym and we are not a lifestyle brand. Come as you are, borrow a mat if you need one, and take your time.

What we teach

Four things, done slowly. Book a single class or buy a class pass.

  1. 01Slow flowGentle linked movement with time to breathe between shapes. Warming, steady and kind to stiff shoulders.
  2. 02YinLong, quiet holds on the floor with cushions and blankets. A good way to end a busy week.
  3. 03Beginners courseFour weeks with the same small group, learning the basics without pressure. Start here if you have never done yoga or want to start again.
  4. 04Pregnancy yogaSaturday mornings, with breathing, gentle strength and plenty of rest. Open to every stage of pregnancy.

Before you come

Questions people ask

I have never done yoga. Can I just turn up?

Yes. The 4 week beginners course is the easiest way in, and any slow flow or yin class is also fine to start with. Tell the teacher it is your first time and we will look after you.

Do I need to bring a mat?

Bring your own if you have one. If not, tell us when you book and we will have one ready.

Can I book a single class?

Yes, single classes and class passes are both available. With only 14 mats, booking ahead is wise.

Is pregnancy yoga suitable for me?

The Saturday morning class is made for pregnant people. If you have any concerns, ask your midwife or doctor first and talk to Mei before your first class.
