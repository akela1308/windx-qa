Two services

Cleanup is for data you already have. A prototype is for a task you keep repeating.

Spreadsheet cleanup

Names and dates made consistent across the sheet, and duplicate rows identified so you can see them and decide what to do with them.

Workflow prototypes

A small working example for one repeated task, so you can see it run. We then talk it through before anything is implemented.

See the workflow

How a workflow prototype goes

  1. Discuss a sample

    We look at a small sample of your sheet and the task that keeps repeating.

  2. Review an example

    You get a small working example for that task and look at how it behaves.

  3. Decide what to implement

    We talk it through, and you choose what gets built before any implementation starts.
