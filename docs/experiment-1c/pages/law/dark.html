What we do

Family law

Divorce, separation and the arrangements for your children: where they live, how time is shared, how money is split. We explain the steps in the order they will happen, so you are not surprised by the next letter.

Wills and lasting powers of attorney

A will that says what you mean, and a lasting power of attorney so someone you trust can act for you if you cannot. Both are usually quick to do and much harder to fix later.

Probate

Dealing with the estate after someone has died: the paperwork, the forms for the court, the bank accounts and the property. We tell you what must be done now and what can wait.

How we charge

A fixed fee wherever the work allows it.

Some matters cannot be priced up front, a contested divorce for one. When that is the case we say so, and say how we will keep you informed of the cost.

Before you book

What happens on the call?

You tell us what has happened. We tell you what your options are and whether we are the right firm for you. It takes 20 minutes and costs nothing.

Will I be told what it costs?

Yes. Where we can offer a fixed fee, we give you the figure in writing before any work starts.

I am not sure I need a solicitor.

Then the call is for working that out. Some people finish it knowing they can handle things themselves, and that is a good outcome.

What should I have ready?

Nothing. If you have a letter or a date in mind, have it near you. If not, we will start from the beginning.
