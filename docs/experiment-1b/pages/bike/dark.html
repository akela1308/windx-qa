Old steel frame restoration

Bring us the frame from the shed, the skip or your dad's garage. We strip it back, check every tube and join, repair what needs repairing, and build it up again so it rides the way it was meant to. Slow work, done properly, and no two jobs are the same.

Before you come in

Do I need to book?

No. Turn up and we'll fit you in where we can. Booking a service just guarantees the stand is free when you arrive.

Can I wait while you fix it?

For punctures and brake fixes, yes. Services are usually done the same day.

Will you work on any bike?

Road, hybrid, commuter and courier bikes are our everyday work. If you're unsure about something unusual, call or message us first.

How do I know what a steel frame restoration will cost?

Bring the frame in. We'll look it over and quote you before we start.
