Yoga in a warm room above the bakery

Kiln Yoga is a small studio in Peckham with 14 mats, a wooden floor and big windows. Mei and two other teachers run slow flow, yin, a beginners course and pregnancy yoga on Saturday mornings.

Beginners course

Four weeks in the same small group and the same room. You start from the very first pose and learn the basics at a speed that suits you, with at most 14 people on the mats.

Who is at the front

I'm Mei and I teach most of the classes. With 14 mats I can see everyone in the room and remember what you told me last week. Two other teachers share the timetable with me, and we teach slow flow, yin, the beginners course and the Saturday pregnancy class.

Mei, teacher and owner of Kiln Yoga
