What we teach

  1. 01Slow flowMovement linked to the breath, at a pace where you can still feel each shape. Most of the week's timetable.
  2. 02YinLong holds on the floor, a few shapes per class. The quiet one, good after a hard week.
  3. 03Beginners courseFour weeks with the same small group in the same room, so nobody is guessing what the others know.
  4. 04Pregnancy yogaSaturday mornings, gentle and slow, with room to rest whenever you need to.

The studio from above: the teacher's mat at the front, fourteen mats on the boards, windows along the top wall, loaves on the shelves of the bakery downstairs.

Hello,

I teach most of the classes at Kiln, and two other teachers cover the rest. The studio is up a flight of stairs above a bakery in Peckham. On a morning class the whole room smells of bread.

There are 14 mats and I will not squeeze in a fifteenth. That way I can see everyone, and you can come in on a day when you are not sure about yourself and nobody will mind.

The floor is wood, the windows are big and the room is warm. Bring a water bottle. I will lend you a mat if you do not have one.

See you on the floor,

Mei

Not sure where to begin?

I have never done yoga. Where do I start?

The 4 week beginners course. Same small group, same room, and each week adds a little to the last.

I am pregnant. Is there a class for me?

Yes, pregnancy yoga runs on Saturday mornings. Slow, gentle, with plenty of time to sit down.

Do I have to sign up for months?

No. You can book a single class, or buy a class pass if you know you will keep coming back.

I just want to switch off for an hour.

Try yin. Few shapes, long holds, and nobody will ask you to do anything clever.

Come up the stairs and you will smell fresh bread before you see the mats.
