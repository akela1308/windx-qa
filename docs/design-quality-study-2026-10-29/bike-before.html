A visit to the arch

  1. Turn up or book

    Walk in off Mare Street, or book a slot if you want to be sure we have a stand free.

  2. We look at it with you

    Dami or Rosa checks the bike and tells you what it needs and what it costs before any work starts.

  3. We fix it

  4. Ride away

Two people in a railway arch

Lowgear Cycle Works is Dami and Rosa, working out of a railway arch just off Mare Street in Hackney.

Most of the people who come in are couriers and commuters. They ride every day and cannot be without the bike, so we work to get it back to them the same day.

Then there are the old steel frames. Dami loves restoring them and will happily talk about yours.
