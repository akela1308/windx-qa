What we do

Three areas, done carefully

We keep our work focused so that each person you speak to has done it many times before. Where we can, we quote a fixed fee.

  1. 01Family lawDivorce and separation, and the arrangements for your children that matter most: where they live, how time is shared, how decisions are made. We aim to settle things calmly and without court where that is possible, and we are ready to act firmly where it is not.
  2. 02Wills and lasting powers of attorneyA clear will says what you want and spares your family guesswork. A lasting power of attorney lets someone you trust make decisions for you if you cannot. We explain the choices in plain English and put them in writing properly.
  3. 03ProbateWhen someone has died, there is a great deal to do while you are grieving. We deal with the paperwork, the estate and the authorities for you, and keep you informed at each stage so nothing arrives as a surprise.

How it works

What people usually ask us first

I have never spoken to a solicitor. Where do I start?

With a free 20 minute call. You tell us what is happening, we tell you honestly what your options are. There is nothing to prepare and no obligation to go further.

How much is this going to cost me?

We offer fixed fees where we can, and we say so at the start. If a matter cannot be fixed, we explain why and give you the most precise estimate we can, in writing.

What happens after the call?

If you want us to act, we confirm the work and the fee in a short letter. You will know who is handling your matter and how to reach them.

Will I understand what is going on?

Yes. We write and speak plainly, explain each step before it happens, and are glad to be asked the same question twice.

Questions

Before you call

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The first call lasts 20 minutes and costs nothing. It is a chance for you to explain your situation and for us to say how we can help.

Do you always charge a fixed fee?

Not always, but we offer one wherever the work can sensibly be priced in advance. We will tell you at the start which kind of fee applies.

Do I need to bring documents to the call?

No. If you have a marriage certificate, an existing will or letters about an estate to hand, it helps, but it is not necessary.

Can I speak to you about more than one thing?

Of course. Many people need a will and a lasting power of attorney together, and we can look at both in one conversation.

Where are you based?

In Islington, North London. We can also speak by phone or video if travelling is difficult.
