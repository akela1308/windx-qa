Three talks to choose from

Each one runs as a keynote of 20 to 60 minutes, on stage or live online.

How teams make decisions

A look at what happens when a group has to choose: who speaks, which information counts and where the decision is actually made. Suited to audiences who work in teams and want to talk about how choices are reached, not only what they are.

Working through uncertainty

About carrying on with the work when the facts are incomplete and plans keep changing. The talk looks at how people and teams can keep moving before every question has an answer.

Turning ideas into experiments

How to shape an idea into a small test that can be tried, watched and learnt from, rather than debated at length. A fit for events about new ideas, product work or change inside an organisation.

Questions organisers ask

How long is a keynote?

Between 20 and 60 minutes, so a talk can fit a short slot in the programme or fill a full session.

Can Daniel speak online?

Yes. Each of the three talks can be given on stage or live online.

Is there a workshop as well?

A workshop on the same day as the keynote is possible if it is discussed beforehand.

Which talk suits our event?

Choose How teams make decisions if your audience is thinking about how choices get made together, Working through uncertainty for a period of change, and Turning ideas into experiments if the event is about trying new things.
