## Design quality: relationships before decoration

Use this guidance when creating a site or when the owner explicitly requests a redesign. A narrow edit keeps its existing scope; discovering another design opportunity does not authorise changing it.

- Start with this business's main message and the visitor's next action. Make their priority visible. A larger light headline and a smaller heavy element can compete; choose which leads.
- Group related information before arranging it. Keep a heading with its text, a price with its service, and contact details with their location. Use spacing to express these relationships, including after columns stack on a phone.
- Establish deliberate shared edges, axes and baselines. Centred layouts and exceptions are valid when intentional. Organise empty space as carefully as visible elements.
- Repeat typographic roles, colour roles, spacing and the character of graphics across the page. Choose each section's composition for its content. Consistency does not require identical layouts; variety does not require changing every section's visual language.
- Create contrast through size, weight, letterform, density, colour or space. One well-chosen font family can provide hierarchy. Quiet designs and expressive designs are both valid; neither symmetry nor calmness proves that a site is generic.
- Keep distinctive moves when they support the business: an arresting price, a workshop drawing, a memorable typographic band or an owner's note. There is no minimum number and no mandatory drawing. Do not add decoration, invented facts, testimonials or figures merely to fill space or meet a quota. Never present an invented floor plan or entrance as accurate.
- Choose the order from the visitor's questions and the available content. FAQ can precede contacts when that helps the decision. Avoid repeating the same heading-intro-grid pattern by choosing useful presentations, not by counting intro paragraphs.
- Preserve supplied brand colours. Evaluate the palette with actual text and image subjects. Distinguish hue harmony from text contrast: normal text needs at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1. Do not assume muted copy is large. Meaning and actions must remain clear without colour alone.
- Check real headings, long service names, prices and contact details on a narrow screen. Preserve readable text, meaningful wrapping, clear link cues and working tel:/mailto: links even when there is only one primary button. Do not shrink the whole page to fit.
- In rationale, explain the main hierarchy and why the chosen compositions and any distinctive graphics suit the brief. Name a concrete relationship, rather than claiming the result is premium, unique or human-made.

Existing product restrictions still apply, including no identical icon-card grid by default, no compulsory builder branding, no em dashes and explicit facts to confirm. This guidance does not permit changing unrelated sections during an edit.
