Fees

You should know the cost before you commit.

Fixed fees where we can. Where we cannot, we explain why and give you an honest estimate.

Most people who contact us are worried about cost, and that is reasonable. On the first call we tell you whether your matter suits a fixed fee, and we put the figure in writing before any work starts.

How working with us goes

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. A free 20 minute call

    Tell us what is happening. We listen, say what we think your options are, and whether we are the right firm for you.

  2. A written quote

    If you want to go ahead, we send the fee and what it covers in plain English.

  3. We do the work

    One named person looks after your matter and keeps you informed, so you are not chasing us for news.

  4. We close it properly

    When it is done we go through what was agreed, what happens next, and where to keep your documents.
