A 4 week course for your first time on a mat

  1. Week one

    Arrive, find a mat, learn how to stand, sit and breathe. No one asks you to touch your toes.

  2. Week two

    The basic shapes, one at a time, with plenty of time to ask questions.

  3. Week three

    Linking the shapes into a short, slow sequence you can follow.

  4. Week four

    A full gentle class, and a plan for what to try next if you want to carry on.

Hello, I'm Mei

I opened Kiln because I wanted a yoga room that felt like someone's home, not a gym with a mat rental desk. I teach most of the classes myself, and two other teachers cover the rest. We keep it small on purpose. If you come twice, we will know your name. If you have an injury, a bump or a bad week, tell us at the door and we will adjust things for you.

Mei, founder and teacher

Before you come

I have never done yoga. Can I just turn up?

Yes, but the 4 week beginners course is the kindest way in. You start with the same small group each week and learn the basics at an easy pace.

How many people are in a class?

There are only 14 mats, so never more than 14 people. That is why the room feels calm.

Do I need to bring a mat?

Please bring your own if you have one. If you do not, ask us before your first class and we will sort something out.

Can I do yoga while pregnant?

Our pregnancy class runs on Saturday mornings. Tell us how far along you are and we will take it from there. It is always a good idea to check with your midwife or doctor too.

Single class or class pass?

Book a single class to try us out. If you find yourself coming often, a class pass is the better way to go.
