What we teach

Mei teaches most classes, with two other teachers. Book a single class or buy a class pass.

  1. 01Slow flowMoving with the breath at an unhurried pace. Long enough in each shape to actually feel it. Good for most bodies.
  2. 02YinFloor-based, quiet and long-held. Bring your tired self, leave with a looser back and a calmer head.
  3. 03Beginners courseFour weeks with the same small group, so you learn the basics without hurrying or feeling watched.
  4. 04Pregnancy yogaSaturday mornings. Gentle movement, breathing and rest, with other people who are also expecting.

The room, drawn to scale-ish. Fourteen mats, big windows along the top, and the door at the back.

Hello, and welcome upstairs.

Kiln is a small studio above a bakery in Peckham. I kept it to fourteen mats on purpose, so that the teacher can see everyone and nobody gets lost at the back.

I teach most of the classes myself, with two other teachers I trust. We go slowly. The room is warm, the floor is wood, and on early mornings the whole place smells of bread.

If you have never done yoga, start with the four week beginners course. If you are pregnant, come on a Saturday morning. Either way, you are welcome here.

See you on the mat,

Mei

Before you come

Can I book a single class?

Yes. You can book one class at a time, or buy a class pass if you plan to come often.

I have never done yoga. Where do I start?

Take the four week beginners course. It is a small group that stays the same, so you can learn at your own speed.

Why only 14 mats?

So the classes stay small and the teacher can see and help everyone in the room.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes. Tell us if you need to borrow a mat when you book.
