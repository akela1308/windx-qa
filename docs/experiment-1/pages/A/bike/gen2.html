03 / Booking

Need it back today?

Can I just turn up?

Yes. Punctures and brake fixes are done while you wait. For anything bigger we will tell you how long it needs.

I ride for work. Can I get it back the same day?

Many of our customers are couriers and commuters, so we plan around that. Book a service and tell us when you need the bike.

How do I book?

Call us or use the button below, say what the bike needs and when you can drop it off.

Will I know the price first?

Yes. We look at the bike, give you a price and wait for your yes before we start.

05 / Who we are

Two people, one arch

I'm Dami. Rosa and I run Lowgear from a railway arch in Hackney. We keep bikes moving, from a quick puncture to a full service, and we build wheels properly. But the part I love is bringing an old steel frame back to life. If you have one in a shed, bring it in and let's have a look.

Dami, owner

06 / Questions

Before you come in

Do I need to book?

No. You can turn up. Booking just helps us plan your day, especially for services.

How long does a puncture take?

We do punctures and brake fixes while you wait, so you can stay and watch.

Do you work on old steel bikes?

Yes, and we restore frames too. Bring it in and we will tell you what it needs.

Can you build wheels for my bike?

Yes. Tell us about the bike and how you ride and we will suggest a build.
