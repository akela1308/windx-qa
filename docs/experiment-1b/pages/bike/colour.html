SAME DAY

Old steel, ridden again.

Lowgear is Dami and Rosa, two mechanics in a railway arch in Hackney. Most of the bikes through the door belong to couriers and commuters who cannot be without a bike for long, so we work fast and we work straight.

The part Dami loves most is restoring old steel frames. A tired frame comes in, and after the strip-down, the repairs and a careful rebuild it goes out riding better than it has in years.

Whatever you bring us, you will know what it needs and roughly what it costs before we pick up a spanner.

Fast for the daily bike. Slow and careful for the old steel.
