Three talks

Each one works as a keynote of 20 to 60 minutes. Pick the topic closest to what your audience deals with.

  1. 01How teams make decisionsA look at how a group gets from several options to one choice: who is heard, how options are weighed and how the decision is agreed. For audiences who work and decide together.
  2. 02Working through uncertaintyA talk about carrying on when facts are incomplete and plans keep shifting, and how people and teams can act sensibly without waiting for certainty.
  3. 03Turning ideas into experimentsHow to take an idea and shape it into a small test that can be run, observed and learned from, before anyone commits to the whole thing.

Questions an organiser asks

Stage or screen?

Either. Daniel speaks on stage at your event or live online.

Can we add a workshop?

Yes, if it is discussed. A workshop can run on the same day as the keynote.

Which topic suits our audience?

Decisions suits people who work in teams and have to agree. Uncertainty suits audiences facing change. Experiments suits those with ideas still waiting to be tried.
