## Art direction (mandatory): Signal colour

Loud and direct. The whole page sits on one saturated colour (tomato red, cobalt, signal yellow, grass green or tangerine), with black or white type, heavy uppercase display, big numbers and blunt copy. Feels like a shop sign.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 38-68%, saturation 55-100%.
- displayFont one of: unbounded, big-shoulders, archivo, syne, bricolage. bodyFont one of: work-sans, public-sans, space-grotesk, ibm-plex-sans, karla.
- radius 0-10px.
- displayCase: uppercase.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.type-poster, hero.band, services.index, signature.big-word. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
