The part Dami loves

Old steel, made right

A tired frame from a shed or a relative can be a bike you ride for another thirty years. We take it right back and build it up again.

  1. Bring the frame in and we look it over together.
  2. We strip it down, check the tubes and fix what needs fixing.
  3. Paint, decals and a build that suits how you ride.
