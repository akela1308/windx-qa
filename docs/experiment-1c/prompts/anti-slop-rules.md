## The site must not look AI-made

People now recognise AI-made websites at a glance. Recurring patterns are the problem; no particular palette or font class is automatically safe or required. A warm neutral serif page can be just as habitual as a colourful one. Your page is checked for them and sent back if it uses them.

Layout patterns to avoid:
- A small uppercase label above every heading ("OUR SERVICES", "01 / PRICES"). There are no section labels in this system; let headings speak.
- The same section formula repeated without regard to content: heading, grey intro paragraph, grid. Use other presentations when useful; an intro paragraph itself is not a defect.
- A landing-page skeleton chosen by habit: hero, services, "how it works" steps, about, testimonials, FAQ, contact. Build the order from what this business actually has: a price board, a timetable, a room, a first phone call, a person. FAQ is optional; its position depends on the visitor's questions.
- Icons, icon cards, pill badges, sparkle bullets.

Copy patterns to avoid (they are checked automatically):
- "Four jobs, done properly", "Three areas, done carefully", anything "done properly/carefully/right".
- Slogans: "Small on purpose", "Calm over combat", "Plain English, always", "Warm, not glossy", "Fast. Fair. Friendly." (taglines, "X over Y", "X, not Y", triplets).
- Empty marketing words: seamless, tailored, elevate, unlock, peace of mind, hassle-free, no fuss, look no further, whether you're, we believe, your journey, world-class, passionate about.
- Write like the owner talks to a customer at the counter: concrete nouns, real details from the brief (the arch on Mare Street, the bakery smell, the 20 minute call), plain sentences.

Distinctive design moves:
- Surprise is useful when it serves the business: a huge supplied price as graphics, a relevant drawing, a word circled by hand or a contrasting marquee band. Keep expressive decisions that work.
- Choose these moves deliberately. Their count, a drawing's presence, symmetry or calmness cannot establish design quality. Apply the design-quality guidance below.
