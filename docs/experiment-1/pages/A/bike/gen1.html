03 / Old steel

The part Dami loves

Most of the bikes through the arch are daily riders, and we keep them moving. But the jobs we look forward to are the old steel frames: the ones with a bit of rust, a dented tube and a story.

We strip them back, look hard at the tubes and joints, fix what needs fixing and rebuild them to ride properly again. Not to sit in a hallway.

If you have a frame in a shed or under a stairwell, bring it in or send us a photo. We will tell you honestly whether it is worth saving.

05 / Questions

Before you come

Do I need to book?

Not for punctures and brake fixes, just turn up. For services, wheel builds and frames, booking helps us hold a slot for you.

Can I get my bike back the same day?

Usually yes for punctures, brakes and services if you drop it in the morning. Tell us when you need it and we will say straight away if we can.

Do you work on any kind of bike?

We mostly see commuter, courier and road bikes, plus old steel frames. Ask us if you are unsure about yours.

How do I get a price for a restoration?

Bring the frame in or send photos. We will look at it and give you a figure before any work starts.
