Beginner hand-building session

Hand-building means shaping clay with your hands and simple tools. The session is made for first-timers, so you need no experience and nothing to bring. You work at your own pace on one simple vessel.

£45

Also possible

  • Private group session

    Coming together with friends or colleagues? A private session can be discussed. Price and dates are not set yet.

Two hours, three parts

  1. Get to know the clay

    Feel how it presses, stretches and holds a shape before you make anything.

  2. Form a simple vessel

    Build your piece by hand, pinching and smoothing it towards a bowl, cup or small vase.

  3. Choose a finish

    Decide how the surface should look and feel, from soft and matt to something glossier.

Pinch, press, smooth: your hands make one simple vessel .
