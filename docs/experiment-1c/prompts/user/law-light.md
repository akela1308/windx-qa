We are Hartwell & Okafor, a small law practice in Islington, two partners, Clare Hartwell and Tunde Okafor, plus a paralegal. We do family law (divorce, separation, arrangements for children), wills and lasting powers of attorney, and probate. Most clients come to us at a hard moment and are worried about cost, so we offer fixed fees where we can and a free 20 minute first call. We need a website that feels trustworthy and calm, explains clearly what we do and how working with us goes, and gets people to book that first call.

## Art direction (mandatory): Newsprint

A local newspaper or printed bulletin. Pure white or barely grey paper (not cream), black ink, one restrained spot colour. Serif headlines, ruled lines between items, numbered labels, columns of text, square corners. Information-dense and trustworthy.

Hard limits checked by the system:
- Page background (tokens.colors.bg): HSL lightness 95-100%, saturation 0-8%.
- displayFont one of: newsreader, source-serif, dm-serif, instrument-serif. bodyFont one of: source-serif, newsreader, public-sans, libre-franklin, ibm-plex-sans.
- radius 0-2px.
- Compositions that express this direction well: hero.editorial, services.ledger, services.table, story.longform, proof.quote-columns. Use them where they fit the content.

Make this direction serve the business. Do not water it down toward a neutral beige page.
