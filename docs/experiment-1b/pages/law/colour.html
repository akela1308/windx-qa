What you might be wondering, and what happens

I do not even know where to start.

That is normal. Book the free 20 minute call and tell us what is going on. You do not need documents or the right words.

How much is this going to cost?

Before any work begins we tell you the fee in writing. Where the work allows it, the fee is fixed, so there is no running meter.

What do you need from me?

We tell you exactly which papers to find, and we ask for them in small lists rather than all at once.

Who will I be speaking to?

One of the two partners handles your matter, with our paralegal helping on the paperwork. You know who to call.

Dear reader,

Most people who contact us have never needed a solicitor before. They are tired, often anxious, and usually worried about what this will cost.

We set up Hartwell & Okafor to be the practice we would want to call ourselves: small, direct and clear about money. You speak to the partners, not to a call centre.

If we are not the right people to help, we will say so on the first call, and you will have lost nothing.

Quick answers

Is the first call really free?

Yes. It lasts 20 minutes, there is no obligation, and we will tell you honestly whether and how we can help.

Do you offer fixed fees?

Where we can, yes. We will tell you at the start whether your matter can be done for a fixed fee and what that fee covers.

Which matters do you take on?

Family law (divorce, separation and arrangements for children), wills and lasting powers of attorney, and probate.

Can I bring someone with me?

Of course. Many people find it easier with a friend or relative beside them.
