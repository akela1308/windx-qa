What we teach

Four kinds of class, all in the same room, all capped at fourteen people.

Slow flow

Movement linked to breath at an easy pace, with time to settle into each shape. You will not be rushed, and nobody is keeping score.

Yin

Long, quiet holds on the floor with the lights low. Mostly about letting go of effort and staying still for a while.

Beginners course

Four weeks with the same small group, learning the basics at a gentle pace. Come as you are, no experience needed.

Pregnancy yoga

Saturday mornings, with support and props for a changing body. A calm hour with other people at a similar stage.

If you have never done this before

I am not flexible. Will I be the only one?

No. Most people in the room cannot touch their toes. We show you how to use blocks and blankets so each shape works for your body.

What do I bring?

Just yourself and something comfortable to move in. We have mats, blocks and blankets in the room.

Will I be lost in a big class?

Fourteen mats means the teacher can see everyone. In the four week course you keep the same small group, so faces become familiar.

Can I just try one class first?

Yes. Single classes are open to everyone, and a class pass is there if you decide to stay.

Before you come

Do I need to book?

Yes, please book ahead. With only fourteen mats, classes can fill up, especially on Saturday mornings.

What is the difference between a single class and a class pass?

A single class is one visit. A class pass gives you several visits to use as you like. Ask Mei for the current details.

Is pregnancy yoga suitable at any stage?

It is designed for pregnancy in general. Please tell Mei how far along you are and check with your midwife or doctor if you have any concerns.

Where do I go when I arrive?

Look for the bakery on the ground floor and take the stairs up. The studio is the room at the top.
