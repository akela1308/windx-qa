Three areas of work, done carefully

We keep our practice narrow on purpose. Each matter is handled by a partner, with our paralegal keeping paperwork moving.

  • Divorce and separationEnding a marriage or civil partnership, agreeing how to divide money and property, and keeping the process as calm as it can be.
  • Arrangements for childrenAgreeing where children live and how time is shared, by agreement where possible and through the court only when needed.
  • WillsWills written in plain English, so your wishes are clear and your family is protected.
  • Lasting powers of attorneyChoosing someone you trust to make decisions for you if you are ever unable to, and putting it in place properly.
  • ProbateHelping executors and families deal with an estate after a death, step by step and without jargon.
  • First call20 minutes · FreeA free conversation to hear your situation and explain your options and likely costs. No obligation.

Where we can, we quote a fixed fee before work begins. Where a matter cannot be fixed, we explain why and give you an estimate.

A small practice, on purpose

Hartwell & Okafor is run by two partners, Clare Hartwell and Tunde Okafor, with the help of a paralegal. We work from Islington and see clients who are, more often than not, meeting a solicitor for the first time.

Most people come to us at a hard moment: a marriage ending, a parent who has died, a decision about the future that cannot wait. Many are worried about what it will cost. That is why we offer fixed fees where we can, and a free first call so you can ask questions before you spend anything.

We would rather explain a thing twice than leave you unsure. We write to you in ordinary language, we tell you what happens next, and we do not hurry you.

Questions people ask before they call

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The first call lasts 20 minutes and costs nothing. There is no obligation to instruct us afterwards.

Do you offer fixed fees?

Where we can, yes. We will tell you on the call whether your matter can be done for a fixed fee, and put the figure in writing before we start.

What areas of law do you cover?

Family law (divorce, separation and arrangements for children), wills, lasting powers of attorney, and probate. If your matter is outside these, we will say so and point you elsewhere.

Who will I speak to?

A partner, Clare Hartwell or Tunde Okafor. Our paralegal helps with documents and keeping your matter moving.

Do I need to come to the office?

You are welcome to, and the office is in Islington. Please ask when you book if you would prefer to talk by telephone.
