Couriers, commuters and old steel

Lowgear is Dami and Rosa, working out of a railway arch in Hackney. A lot of our customers are couriers and commuters who cannot be without their bike for long, so we work to get it back to you the same day.

We do the everyday jobs well: servicing, punctures, brakes. We also build wheels properly, by hand, and we restore old steel frames. That last one is the part Dami loves, and it shows.

Come in, show us the bike, and we will tell you straight what it needs.

Before you come

Do I need to book?

No. You can turn up at the arch. Booking a slot just means we have time set aside for you.

Can you fix a puncture while I wait?

Yes. Punctures and brake fixes are done while you wait.

Will I get my bike back today?

For most repairs, yes. Wheel building and frame restoration take longer and we will agree a time with you.

Do you work on old steel bikes?

Yes, and we love to. Bring it in and we will talk through what it needs.
